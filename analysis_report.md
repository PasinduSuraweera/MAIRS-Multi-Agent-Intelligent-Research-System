- AI adoption in Sri Lankan startups is gaining momentum, transforming industries such as fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and agriculture.
- The growing use of AI and automation in various industries could lead to significant job losses, but new positions are emerging in areas like fraud analytics, AI governance, data engineering, and customer experience personalization.

The trend in Sri Lanka's AI industry is towards rapid growth and adoption, driven by the government's focus on digital transformation and innovation. The implications of the growing AI industry in Sri Lanka are significant, with both positive and negative impacts on the job market. On the other hand, the automation of various industries could lead to significant job losses, particularly in sectors where tasks are repetitive or can be easily automated.
