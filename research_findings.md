The AI industry and job market in Sri Lanka are growing rapidly, with a focus on digital transformation and innovation. The country aims to increase its digital economy's contribution to GDP from 3-5 percent to 15 percent over the next five years, with a strong emphasis on artificial intelligence as a core growth driver. However, the growing use of AI and automation in various industries could lead to significant job losses, and it is essential to address the challenges and opportunities presented by AI in the job market.

Key findings:
- Sri Lanka targets fivefold digital economy growth in five years with AI-centric strategy.
- The country's IT sector is set to become a $3 billion export revenue generator by 2024 and a $15 billion contributor to the economy by 2025.
- AI adoption in Sri Lankan startups is gaining momentum, transforming industries such as fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and agriculture.
- The growing use of AI and automation in various industries could lead to significant job losses.
- New positions in fraud analytics, AI governance, data engineering, and customer experience personalization are emerging.

Important statistics:
- Sri Lanka's digital economy is expected to grow from 3-5 percent to 15 percent of GDP over the next five years.
- The IT sector is expected to become a $3 billion export revenue generator by 2024 and a $15 billion contributor to the economy by 2025.
